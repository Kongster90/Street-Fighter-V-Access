What's new in Street Fighter V Access

Each test version, newest first. The version you have is in VERSION.txt, beside this file.


5 October 2026

The mod now reads everything from the game itself. Reading the screen, which it fell back on when it could not, is gone, along with F9, which switched between the two. Nothing changes in use, and the download is smaller.
New key, Alt C: says what the buttons on the current screen do, from the game's own button hints, in the button style you chose with Alt B. For example, on the main menu: "Options, Fight Request OFF. square, Fighter Profile".
Alt T now has three settings: subtitles off, on (who speaks and what they say), and speaker names only, which says just who is speaking as each line comes.
Fights: the health beeps, counter hit and crossup sounds now play in replays and in story fights too, on the right sides whether you play on the left or the right. In a replay, Alt H names the fighters Player 1 and Player 2.
Crossups: they now sound for fighters in costumes that once went silent, no longer sound when a throw sends someone behind you, and sound only on the hit that starts a combo, not on every hit of it.
Fight Money is named wherever the game shows only a number: your total at the top of every menu (Alt A), mission rewards, shop prices and purchase prompts. Offline, your total is said as "Fight Money unavailable offline".
Main menu: the Login icon says "Logged in", or "Login. Not logged in" when you are offline.
Missions (Challenges): each mission is one sentence, such as "Perform a normal throw 10 times! Progress 0 of 10. Reward 50 Fight Money. 3 hours 46 minutes remaining". Alt R adds the deadline and when it started.
Shop: the item you are on is the one named; one stage you did not own was named wherever you were. Prices are said in Fight Money.
Shop, Fighting Chance: the purchase prompt says its question, the price and your balance before and after, then the button; paying with Fortune Tickets says tickets. The cutscene's button is said ("Next Screen"), and the prize after it: one, with what it is, or all ten, then each by name as you move through them, Alt R giving what each is.
Trials: each trial says its number, whether you have cleared it, and its combo, such as "03. Cleared. Jumping Hard Punch, Standing Medium Punch, Crouching Medium Kick, HADOKEN". Going up the fighter list now says every fighter's progress, not only the name.
CFN, Tournament: each tournament is said in brief, such as "garnetmiki CUP. Over. Entered 4 of 4. Full", with its dates, conditions and rules on Alt R. Triangle's details screen is read as it opens.
Story: General Story's scenes are called scenes, with whether you cleared each on Normal or Extra. Each story says its scene controls the first time you start a scene, and Alt R in a scene says the line showing and the controls. If the game's own subtitles get switched off with square, the mod says so, since nothing can be read without them. The chapter list keeps up as you scroll down it.
Story, Costume Settings: each fighter's name is said as you reach their Costume and Color rows, and square's job, reverting them all, is said on arriving.
Gallery, Good Luck Charms: stay on an item for a second and the mod says what it does. The list no longer repeats its description after every move.
Fixed: garbled characters sometimes said on the main menu, and "www" said in Sound Settings.
The mod stays quick late in a long session; after many hours it used to take a fifth of a second longer to notice each move.


1 October 2026

Fights: a counter hit plays a short sound as it lands, in the speaker on the side of whoever landed it, so you can follow it up. Crush counters are left to the game's own sound. F6 and Shift F6 make it louder or quieter; it starts at 40 percent.
Fights: a crossup, a hit landing from behind, plays a sound of its own as it lands, also on the side of whoever landed it. It plays once for the hit that opens a combo, works in the corner too, and stays quiet for throws. F7 and Shift F7 set its volume, separately from the counter hit's. It is not made in a mirror match, both sides the same character.
The health beeps are now short xylophone notes, a little lower than before, in place of plain tones.
Online fights, in the Battle Lounge and ranked and casual matches: the health beeps now sound there too. They stayed silent, because online the game labels only your own health bar.
Survival and Trials: scrolling down the fighter list says each fighter as you land on them, with their score and time in one sentence. Past the first five rows it used to say nothing until you went back up and down again.
Menus and lists read a little faster, long lists especially.


29 September 2026

Survival: the health you carry into the next stage is now read from the game itself rather than measured off the health bar on screen, so it is right whatever your screen resolution or window. It said "Health 0 percent" after every stage on a 1920 by 1200 screen.
Survival: the stage's sentence is said as soon as the supplement screen appears. It used to wait for the health bar to settle, and was sometimes lost if you moved on quickly.
Survival: a Health Recovery, or a Battle Item that restores health, is said as the next stage starts, when the game applies it: "Health now at 100 percent".
Alt H, during a fight, reads both fighters' health, V-Trigger and Critical Art from the game itself rather than off the screen, so it works whatever your screen resolution or window. It now says how many bars each gauge has, such as "V-Trigger 1 of 2, Critical 2 of 3", and no longer calls a full health bar 83 percent. The first press in a session takes a couple of seconds.
Versus against the CPU from the right-hand side (CPU VS PLAYER 1): the VS screen now names the fighter on the left as your opponent, and Alt H gives your own fighter's health first, as "You". Both assumed you were on the left. The mod asks the game which side you control, so this holds even if the mod was started in the middle of a session.
Fights: a beep as either fighter's health drops past 75, 50 and 25 percent, each lower in pitch than the last, and two short low beeps at 10 percent. Player 1's beeps are in your left speaker and player 2's in the right, as their health bars are on screen. Each sounds once, and again only after health has gone back up, as it does in the next round.
If you had the first zip of 29 September: Alt H and the health beeps could swap the two fighters, in Survival especially. They now tell the fighters apart by character, so they stay the right way round; only a mirror match, both sides the same character, can still confuse them. Survival could also keep saying the health left from an earlier run, the same number after every stage, and a Health Recovery bought just before a stage could go unannounced. Both are fixed.
F5 makes the health beeps louder and Shift F5 quieter, 5 percent at a time, with a beep at the new volume each time. They start at 40 percent, quieter than the first version, and 0 turns them off for anyone who does not want them. The setting is remembered.


24 September 2026

Once the installer closes, it clears out the folder you extracted, leaving only Install SFV Access, Uninstall SFV Access, Read me first.txt, What's new.txt and VERSION.txt. The rest is already in the installed copy, so there is now one copy of the mod, not two. Both batch files still work from the extracted folder. Install asks again whether to start with the game, and Uninstall removes the installed mod.
The mod's own Python now ignores any other Python setup on your computer, so packages you installed for your own Python can no longer stop the mod starting.


23 September 2026

Battle Settings, Random Stage Settings: moving through the stages says each one and whether it is ticked, meaning it can come up when you pick "???" on stage select. Confirm says the new state, stages you do not own say "Not owned", and switching tabs with the bumpers says the tab.
Favorite Stage: switching tabs with the bumpers says the tab.
Random Stage Settings: stages with a second version open a Setting menu with B, and its two choices, DEFAULT and Setting 1, say whether each is ticked.
Replays: the controls are read with their buttons named in your chosen style, such as "L2, Previous Scene. L1 or R1, Change Playback Speed. triangle, Pause", once as each replay begins, and Alt R gives the current ones at any point in the replay.
CFN's timeline, opened with heavy punch in the CFN menu, reads each entry as you move: who, and what they did. Alt R adds when.
A player's Fighter Profile: tables read each row left to right, the fighter first: "KEN. Lv. 12. EXP 750/1600".
A player's Fighter Profile: every page in its lists is read as you move, including Character Level and Fight Money Earned, which were silent. Alt R on a page reads its figures, such as "TOTAL MATCHES: 12426". Character Level no longer reads every fighter's name at once.


22 September 2026

The Message Log reads. Moving through it says what each message says, and Alt R adds when it arrived.
Messages about Fight Money say so. One read "Received 500 ." before, the coin being a picture the mod had no word for.
CFN's menu names each entry, such as "Blacklist" or "Rival Search", before saying what it does. Its entries are pictures with no text of their own, so only the description was read before.
Moving through CFN speaks straight away, without the short wait that was there for menus whose parts arrive a moment apart.
A player's Fighter Profile: on the KO Ratio and KO'd Ratio pages, the fighter and opponent you pick are read as you change them. Alt R says the match-up, such as "ZEKU versus All Characters", and the figures for it.
A player's Fighter Profile: Win Ratio by Character no longer reads out every fighter's name and figures as you move through the pages.
The main menu no longer reads the adverts in its banner every few seconds while you sit on an icon such as Gallery.
Story subtitles, turned on with Alt T, now include General Story's scenes, and what fighters say before a fight in Arcade and Survival.


17 September 2026

The mod's keys only work while Street Fighter V is in front. In any other program they do what they normally do. F10 now closes the mod only while you are in the game.
Arcade: after each fight the result screen says your scores and your total. After the bonus stage it says how many barrels you broke.
Arcade: the card offering the bonus stage is read, before the tips.
Arcade: the VS screen no longer calls the CPU "REWARD", and before the final stage the opponent's card keeps its reward.
Character Story: each chapter is said as a sentence, such as "Chapter 3, Apprentice Alley, versus DHALSIM", and a locked chapter as locked. Alt R adds the fighter's profile.
Options, Screen Settings: Screen Brightness says its level, such as "8 of 10", and each change.
This file, What's new.txt, comes with every version from now on.


16 September 2026

Fixed the mod closing by itself on some computers.
If the mod does close by itself, crash-log.txt in the snapshots folder now says why, however you started it.
Online: "YOU WIN" or "YOU LOSE" is said the moment a match ends, and the result screen says how it went, the room's running score and the rule.
Battle Lounge chat is read as messages arrive.
The VS screen names your opponent whichever side you are on. The mod learns your Fighter ID from the main menu the first time it sees it.


15 September 2026, second version

The installer asks whether the mod should start with the game, and puts a Street Fighter V Access shortcut on your desktop either way.
Survival: between stages you hear the stage you cleared, the health you carry on with, the time, the score for the stage, and the next stage and CPU level. Alt R gives your total score to spend.
Survival: each Battle Item says what it does a moment after its name, and the end of a run says how far you got, your score, and on Alt R every opponent.
Extra Battle: each event is named as you move between them with the bumpers.
The main menu no longer says "Unavailable" when you come back from a mode.
Read me first.txt explains how to update to a newer version.
